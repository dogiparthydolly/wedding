Questions

What should I wear?

Anything springy! Think light colors and florals, with layers, because it gets cool among the redwoods once the sun goes down. The ceremony is outdoors, so wear shoes that can handle grass and uneven ground.

Are kids invited?

We love your little ones, but we've chosen to keep our celebration adults-only. We hope it's a chance for you to enjoy a relaxed night out with us, and we're so grateful for your understanding. Your invitation lists everyone we've saved a seat for.

Are you registered?

Your presence is the best gift. If you'd like to give something, we'd love help with our honeymoon fund. You can send it on Venmo to @dollprat.

Will there be photos?

Yes — we'll have a photographer and content creator capturing the day, and we'll share the gallery afterward.
